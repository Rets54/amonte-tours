Process

How it works

No complications. From "I want to go" to "let's go" — four steps.

  1. 01

    Choose a route

    Browse the catalogue and pick what you want to see. Or write to us — we'll recommend what suits your interests and dates.

  2. 02

    We agree on details

    Departure time, meeting point, preferences on pace and stops. Usually one message is enough.

  3. 03

    Confirm the booking

    We confirm the route and date. Payment upon meeting or in advance — whatever works for you.

  4. 04

    Let's go

    In the morning the guide picks you up from your hotel or a convenient spot. Then — the route, the road, and Montenegro.

Transparency

What's included — and what's not

No hidden costs. You know exactly what you're paying for in advance.

✅

Included

  • Car and driver-guide for the full route
  • Transport throughout the tour
  • Stories about each place — history, context, recommendations
  • Help choosing a restaurant or café
  • Route flexibility during the day
❌

Not included

  • National park entry fees (usually €3–5, paid on the spot)
  • Food and drinks
  • Museum tickets
  • Transfer to airport or outside Montenegro

Entry fees are minimal. We inform you in advance — no surprises at the gate.
